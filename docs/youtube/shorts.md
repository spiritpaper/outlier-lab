# Shorts

Shorts are vertical videos up to 3 minutes long, watched mostly by swiping through the Shorts feed. They work differently from long-form, so judge them separately. Outlier Lab does: Shorts are always scored against a channel's other Shorts.

## How Shorts differ

**YouTube says:**

- **They have their own recommendation system.** YouTube runs separate systems for Shorts and long-form, "optimized for each format". The Shorts feed is "personalized to reflect what we think you want to see next".
- **They're counted differently.** Since 31 March 2025, a Shorts view counts every time a Short starts or replays. The old count is now called **engaged views** in YouTube Analytics. Monetization and Partner Program eligibility still use engaged views. From 24 August 2026, all formats count a view from the first frame.
- **They don't notify subscribers.** Rene Ritchie: "Shorts don't trigger notifications on upload."
- **They earn less per view** than long-form. Shorts get a share of the ads shown between Shorts in the feed.

Because of the counting change, Shorts view counts are much higher than long-form ones for the same attention. Don't compare a Short's views with a long-form video's.

## Making Shorts that work

**Creators say:** Jenny Hoyos, interviewed on YouTube's official blog:

- **You have about one second.** "I really do think you have one second to hook someone, especially on Shorts." Her openings combine surprise, curiosity and a payoff.
- **Build it from small bits**, each a tiny story, so something is always happening.
- **Thumbnails barely matter for reach:** "99.9% of the views are coming from the Shorts feed". They still matter for how your channel page looks.

YouTube's product lead for Shorts, Todd Sherman, pointed in the same interview to creators making even tighter Shorts, around 15 seconds, that capture one moment or one vibe.

**YouTube says** (in its myths post): trending sounds "can increase discoverability, they're not essential", and a phone with basic lighting is usually enough.

## From Shorts to long-form

**YouTube says** Shorts "introduce your content to entirely new audiences", and it hasn't "seen evidence of Shorts negatively impacting long-form performance". It also says not every Shorts viewer will watch long videos. Its guide to turning Shorts viewers into long-form viewers:

1. **Add a Related video link** to the Short (YouTube Studio → Content → the Short → Related video). Link to the long video that goes deeper on the same thing.
2. **Ask in the last 5 seconds**, for example "get the full breakdown by clicking the link right here", with a visual cue toward the link.
3. **Deliver right away in the long video.** Its first 5 to 10 seconds should address the topic of the Short directly: no slow intro, logo or channel update.

**YouTube says:** when Shorts and long-form reach the same audience, keeping them on one channel helps (Todd Beaupré, 2023). The risk creators point out is the opposite case: Shorts on unrelated topics bring subscribers who never watch your long videos.

## Reading Shorts numbers in Outlier Lab

- **Outlier scores** for Shorts are against the channel's Shorts median.
- **My channel** splits views by format (long-form, Shorts, live) in the AI review, so you can see whether Shorts are growing, long-form is, or both.
- The growth blocks count **Shorts uploaded** separately from videos, so you can tell whether more Shorts led to more views.
