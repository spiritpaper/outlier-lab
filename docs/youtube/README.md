## Who's saying it

Advice about YouTube is everywhere, and a lot of it is guesswork. On these pages, every claim says where it comes from:

- **YouTube says**: YouTube's help pages, its official blog, and its staff on the record (Todd Beaupré, who leads growth and discovery; Rene Ritchie, YouTube's creator liaison). This is how YouTube describes its own system. It's the most reliable source on how the system works, though it doesn't explain everything.
- **Creators say**: strategists and creators with a track record, such as Paddy Galloway (a strategist who has worked with MrBeast, Ryan Trahan and Noah Kagan), Jenny Hoyos (Shorts) and a leaked MrBeast production memo. Useful and tested, but it's what worked for them. Treat it as something to try on your own channel.

Every page lists its sources at the bottom. YouTube changes over time, so if something here looks out of date, [open an issue](https://github.com/spiritpaper/outlier-lab/issues).

## The one idea behind all of it

YouTube puts it plainly: **the algorithm follows the audience.** Its help page says the question to ask isn't whether the algorithm likes your video, but "Does my audience like this?". YouTube's recommendations "are driven by what viewers watch and enjoy". Everything else on these pages follows from that.
